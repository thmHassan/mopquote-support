MopQuote

Privacy policy & support

Effective 13 October 2026

What the app stores

MopQuote stores the business name, service prices, customer details, scope notes and quotes you enter on your device so you can create and reuse estimates. The app has no account and no developer-operated server.

Collection and sharing

The MopQuote developer does not receive your quotes or customer details. The app does not include advertising or analytics SDKs. When you choose Share, your device opens its system sharing menu and sends the quote text only to the app or person you choose. That destination handles the shared text under its own privacy terms. Your device's backup settings may include locally stored app data.

Security and retention

Data remains in the app's private storage on your device, subject to your device's security and backup settings. Saved quotes remain until you delete them, use My prices → Delete all app data, or remove the app. You can delete a single quote from the Saved tab.

Contact

For privacy questions, support or a request about your data, open a support request here. Do not include customer names or quote details in a public request. The developer cannot retrieve or delete data stored only on your device; use the in-app controls to remove it.

Updates

If the app's data handling changes, this page and the in-app policy will be updated before the relevant release.
