About MopQuote

Privacy policy & support

Updated 15 November 2026. Android version 1.1.1.

Publisher: YOURRIDE (SMC-PRIVATE) LIMITED. Contact: hassan@mapifyit.com.

Your work stays on your device

MopQuote stores the business name, room and service prices, customer details, scope notes, saved quotes and selected branding images you enter in the app’s private storage. It has no online app account or developer-operated work-record server. We do not receive those records.

Sharing, printing and backups

Sharing, PDF export, printing and manual backup export send selected records to the application or location you choose. That service handles the files under its own policy. Exported ZIP backups are not encrypted by MopQuote and may contain customer information. Keep them somewhere private. Copies saved outside MopQuote, device/computer backups and printed reports are separate from the app’s local storage. Uninstalling can remove local work.

Google Play purchases

Google Play handles payment information and the optional one-time Pro upgrade. MopQuote checks Google-signed purchase records locally and saves a receipt on the device for offline access. It refreshes ownership through Google Play when available and offers Restore purchases. There is no RevenueCat account or developer billing server. Your quotes, photos and customer details are not sent for billing. Google retains purchase and payment records under its own policies; refunds are handled through Google Play. An internet connection is needed to buy or restore. Android and Apple purchases are separate.

Advertising and analytics

This Android edition makes no advertising requests and does not initialize an app-usage analytics service. Google’s advertising library is included but advertising remains disabled. Any future ads-enabled edition will update this policy and show applicable privacy choices before requesting ads.

Images and permissions

You select an optional business logo using the system picker. The selected image is copied into app storage. The app does not require contacts, microphone or precise location access. Internet access supports Google Play purchases and restoration.

Retention and deletion

Records remain until deleted or the app’s data is removed. Deleting an individual record removes it from the current view; a previous local snapshot or unused image may remain until you use Delete all local work in Settings. That action removes current and previous work snapshots and managed images. It does not cancel a purchase or delete Google’s transaction records. Exported/shared copies and external backups must be deleted separately. Support cannot retrieve or remotely delete records held only on your device.

Support and children

Contact hassan@mapifyit.com for help or privacy questions. Only send the information needed to resolve your question; do not send customer records unless necessary and authorized. Support correspondence is handled to answer your request and retained only as needed for support and applicable obligations. MopQuote is intended for adult business users, not directed at children.

Updates

This policy will be updated when data practices change. Read Google’s privacy policy for Google’s services.
