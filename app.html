For independent cleaners

Quote only the rooms your customer wants cleaned.

MopQuote turns your own room and service prices into a clear, itemised cleaning estimate. Choose the work, check the total, then save or share the quote from your phone.

Release status: The Android, iPhone and iPad apps have been built and tested. They are not yet available in the app stores.

How MopQuote works

1. Set your prices

Confirm or change the example rates, and add the services you offer. MopQuote does not choose a market rate for you.

2. Choose the scope

Add only the requested rooms and services. Include travel, other agreed work and notes where needed.

3. Save or share

Review the itemised total, save the estimate on your device or send its text through your phone’s sharing menu.

What does a partial-home quote look like?

If a customer wants one kitchen, one bathroom and two bedrooms cleaned, you can include those rooms only. With example prices, the estimate would read:

WorkExample amount
Kitchen × 1£20
Bathroom × 1£25
Bedroom × 2£30
Total estimate£75

These figures demonstrate the calculation; they are not recommended cleaning rates. Set your own prices and confirm the job’s condition and scope before sending a quote.

Common questions

Can I quote a few rooms instead of a whole house?

Yes. Select only the requested rooms or services. Unselected work is left out of the estimate.

Does MopQuote tell me what to charge?

No. The starting prices are examples. You must confirm or change your own prices before saving or sharing a quote.

Can I add extras and explain what is included?

Yes. Add travel or other agreed work and write scope notes. The shared quote includes the itemised estimate and notes.

Do I need an account or internet connection?

No account is needed to build and save quotes on your device. Sending a quote may require internet access in the messaging or email app you choose.

Where are customer details stored?

Saved quotes and prices stay in the app on your device. The developer does not receive them. Read the privacy policy for details about sharing and deletion.

Support

For an app issue or feedback, open a support request. Do not include a customer’s private details in a public request. A direct support email will be added before store submission.
